# Tactical Elegance: OuiRise PRD

This document defines the strategic alignment between our street-tactical design language and boardroom legacy for OuiRise.

## 1. Color System

- **Champagne Gold**: `#FFD700` (for accents, highlights)
- **Tactical Maroon**: `#8B0000` (for critical elements, underdog legitimacy)

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings**: Bebas Neue (Bold, 28px)
- **Body**: Playfair (Regular, 16px)
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono (Monospace, 14px)

## 3. Spatial Logic

- **Grid**: 12-column system with 16px gutters
- **Breathing Room**: 24px minimum between elements

## 4. Critical Opp Cost Decision

> **Underdog Legitimacy**: We avoid dark mode on consulting side to maintain visual trust. Our color system is designed to be accessible while preserving tactical clarity. *This is the sovereign treasury of our brand.*

## 5. Microcopy Standards

- **Access Granted**: "ACCESS GRANTED" (Champagne Gold, 24px)
- **Catalyzing**: "Catalyzing Community Transformation" (Tactical Maroon, 18px)

This PRD ensures our design language is both street-tactical and boardroom-ready.
